# Enterprise infrastructure and technology delivery — 17-year journey

## Backup, recovery and business continuity

**Business requirement.** Maintain recoverable data and predictable backup operations for critical banking applications while meeting retention and operational requirements.

**Technical environment and work.** Veritas NetBackup, EMC Data Domain, Oracle workloads, Linux/Unix and Windows server estates, storage capacity, backup schedules, recovery objectives and cross-team diagnosis. Work involved backup architecture reviews, data growth estimates, network and server compatibility discussions, operational troubleshooting and coordination with DBAs, storage, network and application owners.

**Problem-solving.** Investigated recurring backup failures and product/infrastructure bottlenecks; coordinated corrective configuration and operational improvements, restore planning and prevention measures. The source career document reports backup success improvement from approximately 65% to 99%; the figure is a reported career outcome, not a benchmark reproduced in this repository.

**Business outcome.** Better recovery readiness, lower backup failure exposure and stronger continuity of business-critical applications.

## Oracle Enterprise Manager and database operations

**Business requirement.** Detect performance and capacity problems before they disrupt production banking applications.

**Technical environment and work.** Oracle Enterprise Manager management servers and agents, Oracle databases, CPU and memory utilization, tablespace growth, storage utilization, listeners, monitoring thresholds, alerts and patch/upgrade readiness. Coordinated Oracle DBAs, infrastructure, middleware and operations teams across a reported estate of approximately 250–300 production databases.

**Problem-solving.** Evaluated monitoring gaps and capacity risks; implemented monitoring coverage and alert thresholds, reviewed growth trends, planned upgrades and escalated production incidents across technical owners.

**Business outcome.** Earlier issue detection, capacity planning before constraints became incidents, and more predictable production operations.

## Customer service platforms and production modernization

**Business requirement.** Keep contact-center operations reliable through infrastructure upgrades, application changes, vendor dependencies and production releases.

**Technical environment and work.** Genesys contact-center platforms, IVR, workforce management, call recording, Oracle databases, infrastructure monitoring, application compatibility, UAT, release windows, rollback planning and post-release support. Coordinated engineering, QA, operations, business teams and vendors.

**Problem-solving.** Translated operational needs into requirements and release plans; resolved technical dependencies and production blockers, led readiness reviews, UAT coordination, go/no-go discussions and incident escalations.

**Business outcome.** Source career material reports 99.95% availability, 45% reduction in MTTR, 99% SLA adherence and 10% CSAT improvement across relevant initiatives. These are historical self-reported outcomes rather than independently reproduced measurements in this repository.

## Vendor and project coordination in practice

Technical delivery required coordinating OEM support, licensing and product compatibility, procurement and environment readiness, change approvals, acceptance testing, escalation ownership and transition to operations. Tools and practices included Jira, Confluence, Agile/Scrum, RAID logs, stakeholder reviews, executive reporting, UAT and change governance.
