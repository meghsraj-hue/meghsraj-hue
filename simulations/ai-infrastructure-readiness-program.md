# AI Infrastructure Readiness — Proposed TPM Simulation

**Status: Conceptual portfolio exercise, not an executed AI Factory deployment or employer project.**

## Scenario
A fictional organization plans to deploy an AI inference service. The TPM must coordinate infrastructure and operational readiness across compute, storage, networking, platform, security and support teams.

## Example workstreams
1. Compute: confirm server/GPU requirements, power, capacity and provisioning ownership.
2. Storage: confirm dataset access, throughput, retention and backup requirements.
3. Network: validate connectivity, firewall changes, segmentation and latency requirements.
4. Platform: agree on deployment, observability and rollback procedures.
5. Security: coordinate access controls, data handling and privacy review.
6. Support: document incident ownership, escalation, spares/RMA contacts and operational handover.

## Sample exit criteria
- Dependency tracker reviewed and assigned
- Performance acceptance criteria defined by engineering
- Security/privacy review completed
- Monitoring and incident runbooks approved
- Support and escalation owners identified

## Deliverables
Program charter, RAID log, milestone plan, readiness checklist, stakeholder review template.
